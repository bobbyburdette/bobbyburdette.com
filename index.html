Bobby Burdette

Director of UX & Creative Strategy, Thermo Fisher Scientific

I run the Creative Innovation Studio at Thermo Fisher Scientific, the in-house agency behind the Fisher Scientific channel. Brand, digital and commerce for a business that serves labs, hospitals and classrooms.

My job is to set the bar, build the team that clears it, and make the business case for design in rooms that would rather talk about spreadsheets.

Before Thermo Fisher I worked at Marc USA, DICK'S Sporting Goods, PNC and Michael Baker International. Agency, retail, banking and engineering. That range is why I'm comfortable in any room, with any stakeholder.

WVU '98. MS in UX Design from Kent State. Born in Beckley, West Virginia, and based in Carnegie, just outside Pittsburgh.

Off the clock it's golf, the Dead, good bourbon and a well-packed pipe.
